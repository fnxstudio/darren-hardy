A Personal Word

Why I Built ELITE.

You're not here for encouragement, so I'll skip it.

You've built something most people never will. You know how to make revenue happen. You know how to solve whatever the market puts in front of you.

Which is why I want to talk about the part that doesn't yield to any of that.

In 35 years of sitting with builders, I have almost never watched someone stall on strategy. I watch them stall on people. The business outgrows the number of good decisions one person can make in a week. Approvals pile up. Talented people wait instead of deciding. Growth arrives, and profit doesn't follow it.

Every real ceiling I have seen is a people ceiling.

Clearing it is different work from the work that got you here. It is slower. It is less visible. It rarely shows up in a quarter. And there is almost no one at your level to think it through with — your team can't be your sounding board, most of your peers aren't operating at your scale, and the advice industry is full of people who have never run anything.

So I built a small room.

ELITE takes fewer than 20 builders a year on a two-year engagement. You get direct access to me, including the only one-on-one time I give anyone. You get a confidential council of founders and CEOs who operate where you operate and will tell you the truth. And you get my training systems installed into your team, not just into your notebook.

It stays at 20 because that's the number where I can actually know your business.

If that's the work you're ready to do, ask my team for a private conversation. There's no pitch on the other end of it. We'll both find out quickly whether this is a fit.
