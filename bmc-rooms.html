How It Works

Three Steps To The Room

A Hosted Room is simple by design. You bring your BMC ticket. A local host brings the room, the screen, and the people.

STEP 01

Reserve Your BMC Seat

Every attendee holds their own Business Master Class ticket — that’s your access to the live program. If you’re not registered yet, grab your seat first, then come find your Room.

STEP 02

Join A Room In Your City

Browse the Rooms below and request a seat with a local host. They’ll confirm the venue, the schedule, and what to bring. Seats are limited by the room — the earlier you claim yours, the better.

STEP 03

Watch Together. Build In Person.

Show up. Watch all three days on the big screen with your local tribe. Debate the ideas, work the exercises together, and leave with a network of driven builders in your own backyard.

Good Questions

Before You Claim A Seat

Do I still need my own Business Master Class ticket?
Yes. A Hosted Room is where you watch the live program — not a substitute for it. Every attendee holds their own BMC ticket. The Room simply adds the in-person experience on top of the seat you already have.
Is the virtual event somehow “less than” being in a Room?
Not at all. Business Master Class was engineered for the screen, and the vast majority of members will experience it virtually — and get everything the program promises. A Room is for builders who simply want more than the program: the people, the debate, and the relationships that come from watching it side-by-side.
Who runs these Rooms?
Members of the community — not Darren Hardy and not the A-Team. Each Room is opened, hosted, and run by a fellow BMC|COLLECTIVE member who wanted to experience the event with others in their city. It’s grassroots by design.
What does it cost to attend a Room?
That’s set by each host — some Rooms are free gatherings, others cover a shared venue, meals, or lodging. When you request a seat, your host will walk you through exactly what’s included before you commit.
My city isn’t listed. Now what?
Two options: watch a nearby Room fill up and grab a travel seat, or open your own. Hosting is easier than you think — start here and we’ll help you get it off the ground.
