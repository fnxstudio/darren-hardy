Good Questions

Before You Claim A Seat

Do I still need my own Business Master Class ticket?
Yes. Going in person is where you experience the live event, not a substitute for it. Every attendee holds their own BMC ticket. The in-person experience simply adds to the seat you already have.
Is the virtual event somehow “less than” being there in person?
Not at all. Business Master Class was engineered for the screen, and the vast majority of members will experience it virtually, and get everything Business Master Class promises. Going in person is for builders who simply want more than the event itself: the people, the debate, and the relationships that come from experiencing it side-by-side.
Who hosts these?
Members of the community, not Darren Hardy and not the A-Team. Each one is opened, hosted, and run by a fellow BMC|COLLECTIVE member who wanted to experience the event with others in their city. It’s grassroots by design.
What does it cost to attend?
That’s set by each host. Some are free, others cover a shared venue, meals, or lodging. When you reach out, your host will walk you through exactly what’s included before you commit.
My city isn’t listed. Now what?
Two options: grab a travel seat in a nearby city before it fills, or open your own. Hosting is easier than you think. Start here and we’ll help you get it off the ground.
