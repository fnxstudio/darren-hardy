What this is

The BMC Legacy Scholarship

Every year, a small number of business owners are given something that is almost never given: a full seat inside Darren Hardy's Business Master Class, the program he built for people serious about growing a business that lasts.

You cannot buy your way in, and you cannot apply on your own. Every recipient is nominated by someone who has been through BMC and believes in where they are headed. This round, three people will be chosen.

Darren created the Legacy Scholarship on a simple belief: the right guidance at the right moment can change the entire trajectory of a business, and the people who would do the most with it should not be kept out by the price of the ticket. It is given because of who you are and how you lead, not the size of your budget.
