What Happens In The 30 Days After.

The members who compound fastest after Business Master Class are never the ones who came by themselves. They are the ones whose key people heard the same thing, in the same room, at the same time.

Three days at BMC produce decisions. Those decisions then have to survive contact with everyone who was not there. That handoff is where most of the value quietly leaks out.

If you come alone
  • You take the notes. All of them.
  • You get home and book 90 minutes to explain three days.
  • Your team receives a summary of a summary.
  • Every plan routes back through you for the missing context.
  • Week one is spent translating. Execution starts in week four.
If you bring your people
  • They hear it from Darren, in the room, unfiltered.
  • The decisions get made on day two, together, before anyone flies home.
  • Nobody needs the context explained. They were there.
  • You stop being the only person who can move the plan forward.
  • Week one is execution. There is no week-four gap.
