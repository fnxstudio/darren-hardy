Every business that compounded across a decade made one key hire. The pattern shows up at every revenue level. The leader who recognizes it first wins the next decade.

Apple · Tim Cook

Market Cap From One Key Hire

In 1998, Steve Jobs hired Tim Cook out of Compaq to lead operations. Cook installed the supply-chain machine that turned every Apple product launch into a global cash event. Cook became CEO in 2011 and led Apple from a $350B company to over $4 trillion. Same vision. Same product line. One hire compounded across two decades.
