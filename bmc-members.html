Lisa Good · Alum
The Turning Point · 02

“If We Raise Prices, We’ll Lose Clients.”

Lisa is Vice President of GSG Computers, an IT support company, in a market where someone will always quote the same work for less. Raising rates felt like handing clients to the competition.

Then came one Business Master Class strategy on pricing. GSG went to its existing clients and said it plainly: the service is exceptional, and to keep it that way, the rates are going up.

One client left, and nobody missed that one. Every other client stayed, without a single complaint. Two said it was about time. Then two new clients signed at the higher rates, choosing GSG over cheaper competitors.

Derrick Westbrook · 6X Alum
The Turning Point · 03

“The Market Is Soft. I’ll Wait.”

Derrick’s company had just launched a new design department. Then interest rates moved. Developers slowed their projects down. Some paused them completely.

The pipeline dried up and the new department started burning cash. The obvious moves were to cut it, or to sit tight until the market came back.

Derrick did neither. One principle from Business Master Class: nobody knows you and loves you more than the clients you already have. So Derrick offered design packages to existing clients on the other side of the business. Most of them said yes.
