Chapter 4 · RoutinesFrom relaunchingto finishingThe leader whose team believes what you launch.

Your moment

You've launched the referral program 3 times. The weekly meeting lasted 6 weeks. Your team has seen enough launches to know the drill: nod, wait, and it'll go away.

What we install

The Monday-to-Friday rhythm. Monday sets the plays, with a leadership meeting and team huddles. So what you decide on Monday is done by Friday, every week, and momentum stops leaking out between launches.
