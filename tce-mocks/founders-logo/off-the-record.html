Off The Record: logo sketches

Name option for the new event, with the redaction bar as its design element. Flat type first; the metal finish comes once a direction is picked. Suggested line under it: The book, told straight. It promises everything in the book, in DH's own unfiltered voice. Founders Event sketches

A. Bar as the dividerSimplest

One redaction bar where DH's rule sits, in red, the full width of the name. It reads instantly as "redacted", and the name underneath says what it means. ALL-NEW LIVE EVENT and the dates are part of the lockup, so nobody has to work out that it's an event or that it's new.

B. Name cut out of the barStrongest as a mark

The bar is the logo. OFF THE RECORD is cut out of a solid block, like a label on a classified file. It works the smallest of the three (social avatar, lanyard, stage corner) and holds up in white, red or ink.

C. Compounding redactionsTies to the book

Lines of a page, blacked out, and each one longer than the one before. The run of bars makes the compound curve, turned on its side, and the last full-length bar becomes the rule over the name. It covers off the record, the book and compounding at once. Shown in the jacket's condensed type and in the serif.
