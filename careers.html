Our Manifesto

What We Believe.
What We Don’t.

Required.
  • Audacity.Playing small is a sin against your nature.
  • Obsession.Motivation is weak and passion wanes.
  • Impact.You do make a difference. Make it positive.
  • Action.It doesn’t just happen.
  • Growth.Better is always possible.
  • Accountability.Everything is your fault. Own it.
  • Excellence.We have zero tolerance for mediocrity.
  • Courage.Especially when we’re scared to death.
  • Being Real.We’re all “flawsome.”
  • Being On Time.Because we hate burpees.
Refused.
  • Excuses.Step up or step aside.
  • Blaming.Fix it or zip it.
  • Whining.If you want it, earn it.
  • Regrets.Just do better next time.
  • “Good.”You are here to be great.
  • Motivation.Do it whether you feel like it or not.
  • Dreaming.Unless you back it up with a crazy amount of doing.
  • Talent.Only training and preparation.
  • What’s Popular.Popular is the average. Average is mediocre. We abhor mediocrity.

No dancing, high-fiving, or jumping up and down at seminars or on Zoom. That’s just weird.
