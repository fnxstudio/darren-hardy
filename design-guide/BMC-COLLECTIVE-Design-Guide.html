01 · Foundations

The Principles.

Three rules govern everything. Hold these and the brand can't drift.
Typeface
Inter — everywhere. No second family, ever. Hierarchy is built from weight + size + letter-spacing + color, never new fonts. Weights in use: 400 body · 500 labels · 600 emphasis · 700 names · 800–900 display.
One accent
A single chromatic accent, applied sparingly. cyan on dark backgrounds, plum on light. Gold is reserved for review stars; purple/magenta only for the ambient wash. Everything else is near-monochrome.
Case & space
Display, eyebrows, labels, chips, titles are UPPERCASE; body + quotes are sentence case. Generous space on desktop; tightened & uniform on mobile. Flat, bordered, sharp — borders over shadows.
Prime directive
The brand is implicit restraint. One typeface, one accent, lots of space. If a choice adds decoration instead of clarity, it's wrong.

The one rule
03 · Accent logic

Cyan On Dark. Plum On Light.

The single rule that governs the whole page. The accent is the same idea in both modes — it just changes hue so it stays legible and premium. Cyan washes out on white; plum disappears on black.
Dark surface → cyan
Your Secret Weapon.
On any dark section, emphasis, em.key, titles, and CTAs are cyan.
Light surface → plum
Real Builders. Real Results.
On any light section, the very same role lands in plum instead — readable + premium.
Exception: functional cyan UI that lives on media — play buttons, video controls — stays cyan regardless of the surrounding section, because it sits on imagery.

06 · Spacing, motion & rules

Putting It Together.

Spacing
Container max 1380px, side pad 48px / 24px mobile. Desktop section padding is generous + varied (120–200px). Mobile is uniform 72px top/bottom — open space is the brand, but never sprawl on a phone.
Radius & elevation
One radius: 4px (cards/chips/badges); circles 50%. Prefer borders over shadows — cards go cyan-bordered on hover, not heavy lifts.
Motion
Subtle scroll-reveal (short fade-up, easing cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)). Fires as the top edge enters. Respects prefers-reduced-motion. No bounce, no spin.
Testimonial imagery
Static cards: 3:2, deep-plum bg + soft glow. Video stills: 16:9, cyan→purple duotone, subject left, eye contact, open lower-right for the play button. Head size consistent across every image.

Do

  • Build hierarchy with Inter weight/size/tracking + the one accent.
  • Cyan on dark, plum on light — always.
  • Reference tokens (var(--…)), never raw hex.
  • Let desktop breathe; unify spacing on mobile.

Don't

  • Add a second typeface or a third accent color.
  • Use gold anywhere but stars, or the wash anywhere but backgrounds.
  • Use cyan on light (use plum), or plum on dark (use cyan).
  • Add heavy shadows or large rounded corners.
