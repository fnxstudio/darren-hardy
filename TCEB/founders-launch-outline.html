01

The doctrine

Three rules that govern every tier. They are the reason this is a book launch with an enrollment inside it rather than an enrollment campaign wearing a book.

First contact is news

The first thing any audience receives is that the book exists, what it is, and why it took this long. No price in it. They have been asking him "what about my business?" for fifteen years and this is the answer arriving. Let it be that for one email before it is anything else.

Tell them why they are first

Position only works if it is named. An unexplained early send reads as a send date, not as standing. So every tier is told plainly what earned it: you were in Charleston last week, you have sat in my classes, you are a member, you have carried the first book for years. The reason they are hearing it first is the same reason they are the right person to carry it, which makes the naming the setup for the ask rather than flattery.

Assume drift, never the lapse

A lot of these names have gone quiet, and we know why some of them did. The copy never does. No "we miss you," no "it has been a while," no accounting for the gap in either direction. Treat every quiet name as drift, which is the book's own word for it and carries no blame, then hand them something new. A book they can hold is a better reason to come back than any reckoning, and it lets them return without having to explain themselves.

Founder is a role, not a price

A leader who enrolls before print gets a name in the book and a seat at the making of it. The $4,995 is Darren paying $5,000 of a $9,995 COLLECTIVE year toward a mission, stated as such. Never a discount, an early bird, or a savings.

Decision required before anything is built

The print date lands in the middle of your waterfall

Print is currently November 1, and the Founders names list closes with it. Your release order does not reach DarrenDaily and Compound Effect readers until November 2, the rest of the database until the 9th, and cold until the 11th. As dated, three of your six tiers cannot become Founders at all, and the two largest never get a chance at the name in the book. Tiers 2 and 3 get two days and one day.

The DD series makes that sharper than the dates alone suggest. You tease it the week of Oct 26, inside the Founders window, and then launch it Monday Nov 2, the day after the window shuts. A quarter of a million people get built up all week and meet the book one day too late to be in it.

Which means the single most re-engaging asset in the campaign, a printed page with their name on it, is offered only to the people who were already in the building.

What each tier hears

The angle per audience, not the copy. Every one of these opens on the reader or on the book as news, never on the event, the price or a diagnosis of their business. Each also carries its own reason for hearing it when they did, stated plainly, because standing that is not named is standing that is not felt.

1 · BMC45 attendees
In room Oct 21 · email Sat Oct 24

You have the machine. Here is the manual, and your name goes in it.

Why you are firstYou were in Charleston three days ago. You installed this before anyone else has read a word of it, so you hear about the book before anyone else does.

They are three days off the highest-emotion experience Darren runs. The reveal happens from the stage at the close, the email lands Saturday while the workbook is still on the kitchen table. This is the only tier where the offer can ride in the first contact, because they have already had three days of news.

2 · ELITE, HPF and BMC alumni
Thu Oct 29

The language you already speak, now in print and permanent.

Why you are earlyYou have sat in these classes with me, so you hear about the new one first. New is the word that matters: this is not a recap of what they already own, it is the next thing, built for where they are taking the company from here. ELITEs are named for their standing rather than lumped in with the list.

They know the vocabulary and they know how Darren sells. Lead with the book existing and what is new in it: the five multipliers, Month Thirty-One, the delay. Founder status is the way back into the studio, and the referral link is how they earn one of the forty. A large part of this tier has drifted, and some of the best of them left for a reason we understand: HPF was in person and BMC is virtual, and they wanted the room. Never say that to them. Say nothing about the gap at all. What answers it without naming it is Charleston: forty seats, in person, three days, and an award that is the way to earn one. That is the strongest re-engagement in the campaign and it works precisely because it is an offer rather than an apology. Grandfathered ELITEs sit in this tier and should be identified before the send, since their standing is a benefit and the copy should read as though we know it. Current COLLECTIVE members sit here too and they are a full audience for this, not a suppression: the $4,995 is the event seat. For them the copy should lead on the three days and the role rather than on the membership year, which they already have.
