The goal

Seats at the April event

Everything in this plan exists to get every audience engaged with Darren and with the book. That is the work. The measure is different and narrower: seats enrolled at the Founders Event in April. Those are the Founders.

Engagement is how the number happens, not a substitute for it. A DD pageview, a tool download, a re-activated subscriber and a returned alumnus are all real and all worth having, and none of them is the goal. When a choice comes up between an asset that grows reach and one that enrolls a seat, the seat wins, and where a single asset does both it goes first.

The one exception is the in-studio award, which is a closed track. It runs only to people who already hold a seat and its job is multiplying enrollments through them. It is never an acquisition tool and it never appears in copy written to anyone who has not enrolled.

01

The doctrine

Three rules that govern every tier. They are the reason this is a book launch with an enrollment inside it rather than an enrollment campaign wearing a book.

First contact is news

The first thing any audience receives is that the book exists, what it is, and why it took this long. No price in it. They have been asking him "what about my business?" for fifteen years and this is the answer arriving. Let it be that for one email before it is anything else.

Tell them why they are first

Position only works if it is named. An unexplained early send reads as a send date, not as standing. So every tier is told plainly what earned it: you were in Charleston last week, you have sat in my classes, you are a member, you have carried the first book for years. The reason they are hearing it first is the same reason they are the right person to carry it, which makes the naming the setup for the ask rather than flattery.

Assume drift, never the lapse

A lot of these names have gone quiet, and we know why some of them did. The copy never does. No "we miss you," no "it has been a while," no accounting for the gap in either direction. Treat every quiet name as drift, which is the book's own word for it and carries no blame, then hand them something new. A book they can hold is a better reason to come back than any reckoning, and it lets them return without having to explain themselves.

Status is the engine, and it is never named

Every audience here wants the same thing underneath: to be one of the people seen doing this. Never say so. Stage it. Show real readers with the first book, Founders named for the second, the forty chosen, the top five at dinner, and let the reader supply the wanting. The moment copy says you will be recognised it reads as a promise and the spell breaks. Showing someone else being recognised reads as fact. We hold hundreds of photographs of real people with The Compound Effect, and that archive is the visual spine of this campaign, because the whole argument is that it is happening again and this time your name is in the book.

Founder is a role, not a price

A leader who enrolls before print gets a name in the book and a seat at the making of it. The $4,995 is Darren paying $5,000 of a $9,995 COLLECTIVE year toward a mission, stated as such. Never a discount, an early bird, or a savings.

Decision required before anything is built

The print date lands in the middle of your waterfall

Print is currently November 1, and the Founders names list closes with it. Your release order does not reach DarrenDaily and Compound Effect readers until November 2, the rest of the database until the 9th and cold until the 10th. As dated, three of your six tiers cannot become Founders at all, and the two largest never get a chance at the name in the book. Tiers 2 and 3 get two days and one day.

The DD series makes that sharper than the dates alone suggest. You tease it the week of Oct 26, inside the Founders window, and then launch it Monday Nov 2, the day after the window shuts. A quarter of a million people get built up all week and meet the book one day too late to be in it.

Which means the single most re-engaging asset in the campaign, a printed page with their name on it, is offered only to the people who were already in the building.

The Year-End Audit

The event · Mon Dec 14 to Fri Dec 18

The Compounding Audit, dated to one week. Seven surfaces, one arrow each, one sentence of evidence, run with your leadership team before you close the year, with Darren walking it in one short piece. The chapter stays closed. The tool is already promised free at the book's own download address, so nothing new is given away.

It is an event because it is dated and collective. Everyone runs it the same week, and the DD run, the social and the paid all point at one thing instead of at the offer.

Why it converts

Diagnosis, not content

A leader who has just written STANDARDS, arrow down, with their own evidence beside it is a qualified enrollment in a way no reader ever is. The audit hands them a diagnosis and no fix, which is the exact condition the seat answers, and they worked that out themselves rather than being told.

Withholding the chapter is what makes that work. A free chapter would start explaining, and explaining is what the book and the event are for. Give them the question, not the answer.

Why it survives the holidays

The constraint, answered

It asks for ninety minutes they had already scheduled, in a week they are already reviewing the year, with people already in the building. Nothing to attend and nothing live.

Darren's cost is one short piece walking the seven surfaces, which shoots with the January opener. It is the only thing he has to make all month.

DD series concepts

Five suggestions for the week of Nov 2, none of them chosen. All five are drawn from the book and all five are written to the avatar rather than to the DD audience in general, which is the distinction that matters here: the list is much wider than the buyer, and a concept that plays to the list will fill a week and sell nothing.

Read this before picking

  • Tuesday November 3 is US Election Day. Episodes two and three land on the day and the morning after, which is the noisiest media window of the year. That argues either for a different week or for a concept that turns the noise into its own point. It is the single biggest input into which of these five to run.
  • What they think they want in November is a plan for next year. What moves them is being shown that the plan is aimed at the wrong layer, or being handed their own numbers. Concepts that do neither will be enjoyed and forgotten.
  • The series has to produce a diagnosis, not a case. December and January both depend on people arriving with a problem they found themselves. A concept that argues beautifully and diagnoses nothing breaks the two phases behind it.
01

What Your Company Does When You Leave

Recommended

The operating system made visible through absence.

Mon
4:55 on a Friday and nobody decides.
Tue
Your intentions visit now and then. Your defaults clock in every morning.
Wed
The Frank test. Count the decisions that route through your phone.
Thu
What happens automatically when a customer complains, a deadline slips, a mistake is found.
Fri
The standard you keep where no one is looking is the only standard you have.

Why nowThey are about to leave for the holidays and be quietly afraid of it, so the season supplies the test for free. It diagnoses rather than argues, which is what December and January need. And it is the one concept that survives the election week rather than fighting it: everyone is looking at the news, and here is what your company is doing while nobody is watching.

NoteNever sell this as a business that runs without you. They want to go deeper on what matters, not further away.
