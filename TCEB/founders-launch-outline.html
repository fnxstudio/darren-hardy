01

The doctrine

Three rules that govern every tier. They are the reason this is a book launch with an enrollment inside it rather than an enrollment campaign wearing a book.

First contact is news

The first thing any audience receives is that the book exists, what it is, and why it took this long. No price in it. They have been asking him "what about my business?" for fifteen years and this is the answer arriving. Let it be that for one email before it is anything else.

Position is the reward

Nobody is told their tier. They feel it: earlier audiences simply hear it earlier, and their Founders window is longer. The waterfall is the status mechanism. It needs no copy of its own and it survives being noticed.

Founder is a role, not a price

A leader who enrolls before print gets a name in the book and a seat at the making of it. The $4,995 is Darren paying $5,000 of a $9,995 COLLECTIVE year toward a mission, stated as such. Never a discount, an early bird, or a savings.

What each tier hears

The angle per audience, not the copy. Every one of these opens on the reader or on the book as news, never on the event, the price or a diagnosis of their business.

1 · BMC45 attendees
In room Oct 21 · email Sat Oct 24

You have the machine. Here is the manual, and your name goes in it.

They are three days off the highest-emotion experience Darren runs. The reveal happens from the stage at the close, the email lands Saturday while the workbook is still on the kitchen table. This is the only tier where the offer can ride in the first contact, because they have already had three days of news.

2 · ELITE, HPF and BMC alumni
Thu Oct 29

The language you already speak, now in print and permanent.

They know the vocabulary and they know how Darren sells. Lead with the book existing and what is new in it: the five multipliers, Month Thirty-One, the delay. Founder status is the way back into the studio, and the referral link is how they earn one of the forty. Grandfathered ELITEs sit in this tier and should be identified before the send, since their standing is a benefit and the copy should read as though we know it.

3 · Hardy Club members
Fri Oct 30

You are already inside. This is what was being built in here.

Insider framing, not offer framing. They should feel the membership working: they heard it before the database did, and they see the jacket before it is public. Founder status is the natural next step, not a pitch.

4 · DarrenDaily and Compound Effect readers
Tease week Oct 26–30 · series Nov 2–6

Fifteen years of asking him the same question. Here is the answer.

The biggest emotional payoff in the campaign and the tier most likely to be wasted. These are people who re-read the first book every summer and hand it to new hires in their first thirty days. So this one is not a send, it is a week of DarrenDaily.

The week of Oct 26, while the ELITE, HPF, alumni and Hardy Club tiers are in the promo, DD teases a special series coming. No title, no book, no date. Then the series runs Nov 2 to 6, one idea a morning, with the cover revealed on the Monday. Proposed shape, and Darren owns it: the Big Shiny Lie, the defaults nobody wrote, Month Thirty-One, the penny against the org chart, and the mirror with a payroll. Five mornings, five reps, exactly the way DD already works.

First contact stays pure news. The tool library is the action all week. The seat is a separate send at the end of the week, and only to the ones who engaged with the series.

5 · Ecosphere and the rest of the database
Mon Nov 9

There is a new Compound Effect, and the tools are yours today.

Site takeovers across the properties plus a database send. The conversion goal here is the tool library at the book's own download address, which captures the email and positions the book, rather than the seat. Most of this list will never take a seat and all of it can re-engage with a book.

6 · Cold
Wed Nov 11 onward

Name the symptom. Never the book, never the cause, never a revenue floor.

The only tier where the book is not the opener, because a stranger does not care that an author published. The Big Shiny Lie and Month Thirty-One carry it. Traffic goes to the tool library first, seat second. Paid and remarketing live entirely in this tier.

Open, and who has to answer

01

Print date. Darren.

Section 04. Nothing downstream can be scheduled until this is settled, because it decides whether three of your six tiers have an offer at all.

02

Does Darren reveal from the stage on Oct 21?

The event context file recommends opening sales at BMC45 as the close-of-event offer. Your order puts the BMC45 email three days post event. I have built both: reveal in the room at the close, email Saturday the 24th as the follow-up. If the stage reveal is out, tier 1 needs a different first contact, because "you were there and we said nothing" is worse than not being first.

03

Tier 1 lands on a Saturday.

Three days post an event closing Wednesday is Saturday October 24. For an audience still holding the workbook that is defensible and possibly better than Monday. If you want the weekday, it moves to Monday October 26 and every downstream date shifts two days.

04

Tier 4 to tier 5 gap.

Your note fixes tier 5 at two days before cold but leaves the gap after the DD series open. Set to five days from the series opening, which puts tier 5 on Mon Nov 9, one business day after the series ends, and cold on Wed Nov 11.

05

The DD series has to be finished before BMC45 opens.

Five episodes go live Monday Nov 2. The tease runs Oct 26 to 30, and the two weeks before that are BMC45 and the tier 1 to 3 sends, which will take the whole team. That leaves no production window in late October. The episodes need to be scripted, shot and cut by Friday Oct 16, alongside the BOA and the teaser build. This is the tightest constraint in the campaign and it is a calendar problem, not a copy problem.

06

The tease now runs across the event itself.

Starting Oct 19 means the teaser is live on the properties while BMC45 is in session and while Darren reveals from the stage. That is the right shape, but it makes one thing load-bearing: the teaser must carry no title and no date, or an attendee will see the public site saying more than the room did. Everything specific waits for the close on the 21st.

07

Current COLLECTIVE members.

The seat is a COLLECTIVE year, so a current member is being asked to invest $4,995 in something they hold. They sit inside tiers 2 and 3. Until alumni and member pricing is ruled, they should receive the book announcement and be suppressed from the seat offer.

08

BOA reactions are not testimonials.

Early-reader enthusiasm is a product opinion, not a banked result, and it cannot be used where a testimonial goes. Collect it for headline language and for the launch's excitement assets, and keep it away from the enrollment page's proof section, which needs results with magnitudes attached.

09

The in-studio award still needs a legal read.

The thirty-seat pool drawn among people who influenced ten or more seats is a prize conditioned on a paid seat. Until that is cleared, every asset should describe the forty as merit and selection: top ten win outright, Darren selects the rest by fit. Never contest, never sweepstakes, never given away.
