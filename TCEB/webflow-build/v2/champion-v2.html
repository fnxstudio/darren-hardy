    Questions.Answered.

    Still stuck? Your Champion host is a reply away.

    What counts as an impact?−

    Every leader who takes a seat at the Founders Event because of you. They join through your link, they name you at checkout, or you invest in a seat for one of your own leaders and name them. Each one is one impact.

    Started late. Can I still make the Studio?+

    Yes. Every impact counts the same until the final count, and each sprint of the season opens a new play. Plenty of the board is still to be decided.

    How do I see where I stand?+

    Your Champion Board shows your rank, your impacts and how far you are from your next coin. It updates the moment a leader's seat is confirmed.

    A leader says they joined. Where's my credit?+

    They show on your board as pending right away, and the impact locks in once their seat is confirmed. If it hasn't shown up, reply to any Champion email with their name and we'll sort it out.

    Does my own ticket count?+

    Your own seat is your way in. Impacts are the leaders you bring with you.

    How is In the Studio awarded?+

    By rank at the final count: the top-ranked Champions. Every seat is earned by rank and Darren's selection. Nothing is left to chance.
