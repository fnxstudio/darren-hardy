What we're up against

Your company runs on an operating system. Nobody wrote it.

It's made of the small stuff. How decisions get made. What gets rewarded. What gets let slide. What happens on a Tuesday when you're not in the building.

  • The meeting that ends without a decision.
  • The deal that “needed” 15% off to close.
  • The question only you can answer, asked all day long.

None of it matters on any one Tuesday. But it happens every Tuesday, through every person on your payroll.

So the usual advice shows up. You've got the wrong people. You're the bottleneck. Clean house and start over.

Here's the truth. It's none of those. Your people aren't the problem. And neither are you.

85%Same workers, rehired

GM's plant in Fremont, California was the worst in America. Absenteeism around 20%. Sabotage. Thousands of grievances. GM shut it down.

2 years later it reopened as NUMMI with Toyota and rehired more than 85% of the same workers. Within a year its quality rivaled Japan.

They kept the people. They changed the system.
