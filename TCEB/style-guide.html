01

The zoning rule

This property runs two accents, and the whole system depends on keeping them apart. Red belongs to the book. Gold belongs to the founding tiers. They are warm neighbours, so if they ever share a band as large fills the hierarchy collapses and the page reads as decoration rather than structure. Zone them and they do real work: a reader can tell at a glance whether he is looking at something he buys or something he earns.

Red owns
  • The book itself, the jacket, the cover rule
  • Every primary call to action, on all four sites
  • Moneylines, pull quotes, math boxes
  • Eyebrows and section markers
  • Emphasis inside body copy, in place of italics
Gold owns
  • THE COMPOUND 100 and STUDIO 46, wherever they appear
  • THE FOUNDERS EVENT surfaces and enrollment
  • The pre-print countdown
  • Earned-tier badges and flags
  • Nothing else. Gold is a status signal, not a highlighter.
Never
  • Red and gold as large fills in the same band
  • Gold on a primary CTA that sells the book
  • Red on a founding-tier card
  • Gold used for general emphasis or to make something look premium
  • A third accent. Two is already one more than the franchise had.
When they must meet
  • Gold reduces to a rule, a badge, or a numeral
  • Red stays on the button, gold stays on the flag above it
  • Separate them with a full-width dark band, never a hairline
  • See the tier grid in section 05 for the canonical handling

Typography

Two faces. Oswald carries display, headlines, eyebrows, numerals and buttons. Inter carries body copy, UI and fine print. Oswald is a condensed face and it stops being readable somewhere around 18px, which is exactly why the split exists. Setting a paragraph in Oswald is the fastest way to make this system look wrong.

Oswald Medium (500) is DH's specified weight and does all the display work. Oswald Light (300) is the subtitle voice. It is the only other weight this property uses. Do not reach for 600 or 700.

Inter · body

Lede. Sets up a section and carries the argument. clamp(17px, 1.5vw, 20px) at 1.62.

Body copy. Seventeen pixels at 1.72, set in Inter, colored warm grey rather than neutral so it sits correctly against black. Emphasis inside a paragraph shifts to red rather than italic, which is a rule carried over from the original Compound Effect page and holds here.

A second paragraph sits twenty pixels below the first. Measure stays under about forty characters wide so the eye does not have to travel.

Small. 13.5px. Captions, notes under a price, secondary detail.

Fine print. 12px. Shipping notes, disclaimers, and any link inside fine print stays the line's grey with an underline, never an accent color. Like this one.

The jacket is a CSS object, not a photograph. There is no final cover art yet, and this stands in for it honestly: the type treatment is the approved one, the proportions are a standard trade hardcover, and nothing has been invented. Replace it with the real jacket image the moment one exists.

Do not

Do not generate cover art. Do not add a photographic background, a foil effect, or a mockup of a book on a desk. When the printer's jacket file lands, it drops into .jacket-wrap as a single image and the CSS jacket is deleted.

Do
  • Keep the four title lines in their fixed proportion. They are set as ratios of one font-size.
  • Keep the subtitle in Oswald Light, always two lines, always breaking after "Profits,".
  • Give the lockup clear space of at least the cap-height of COMPOUND on every side.
  • Recolor the rule to red on light grounds using .lockup-dark.
Do not
  • Set the lockup below 26px total. It stops being legible.
  • Change the weights. Medium title, Light subtitle, nothing else.
  • Stretch, condense further, outline, or add a drop shadow.
  • Break "IN BUSINESS" onto its own weight or color it.

Alternate .ground-black and .ground-ink to separate bands without drawing a rule. Use .ground-paper at most twice on a long page, and only where the argument shifts. A moneyline band gets its own full section with nothing beside it.

07

Copy rules

These are not preferences. They come from the manuscript's compliance notes and they apply to every asset built from this property, including this document.

Never
  • No em dashes. Anywhere.
  • Never open on the reader's problem. He built the company. Open on identity or on the cause.
  • Never say "operating system" in a cold headline. Earn it by the third paragraph.
  • Never lead with the tools, the chapters, or the five upgrades.
  • Never promise a number, a growth rate, or a timeline to a result.
  • Never use an undefined "they" as the villain.
  • Never present Beth, Dave or Mike as case studies. They are composites.
Banned words
  • unleash, unlock, unveil, ensure, furthermore, nestled, game changer, quietly, sleepwalk
  • transform used as hype
  • "journey" except in "customer's journey"
  • In BMC-facing copy also: coach, coaching, struggling, overwhelmed, breakthrough, burnout, hustle, inspire, crush it, "operator" as a noun, "already excellent"
  • Exception: "stop chasing the breakthrough, build the company that produces it" is a verbatim quote and stays

08

Open items

Nothing ships to a customer until these are closed. Listed in the order they block work.

Blocks the first launch email

1. The existing-member ruling. Members who paid $9,995 versus founders at $4,995. One answer, decided once, used everywhere. Until it exists, that question is omitted from every FAQ rather than improvised.

2. The COMPOUND 100 pre-print deadline. Currently a placeholder of 1 October 2026 in the countdown, set as a single variable. It has to be real and published early enough for a leader to get a 100-book purchase approved.

3. The guarantee. The Zero-Risk Guarantee is written against a 3-day BMC with a 5 PM PT Day 1 deadline. This event has two main days and a tiered Day 3. No asset carries guarantee language until the re-cut terms exist in writing.

Placeholders currently live in the build

Founders Event dates shown as 13 and 14 April 2027 with Day 3 on the 15th. Provisional.

Bulk pricing is list price at $29.95 with free shipping. No volume discount has been ruled on. If a discount is approved, every tier price and the page math change together.

Advance praise does not exist. The praise module is built and dormant. Franchise proof stands in its place. Do not populate it with anything invented.

Payment plan for the 100-book commitment is undecided. A $2,995 approval cycle is a real obstacle and installments would move the number.

Cover art does not exist. The jacket is a CSS type treatment. No generated imagery, on this or any page.

Structural, from the context file

The book cannot point at the Founders Event. The manuscript only ever prints THECOMPOUNDEFFECT.COM/BUSINESS. That page has to carry the route to the event, and it has to keep working after the founders window closes. Build the offer block there so it can be swapped without touching the URL.

Reconcile the download count. Seven callouts in the book, ten named in Context v4, fifteen tools listed in the back matter. This has to be settled before the tools page ships.
