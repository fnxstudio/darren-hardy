Recommendation: A. Every option above beats gold on contrast, and every one except pure white also survives on a light ground, where gold was impossible at 1.85:1. That constraint is the only reason the founding tiers were pushed onto their own black ground in the first place, so dropping gold loosens the system rather than costing it anything. A two-colour system with one red is stronger than a three-colour one, and the tier is already marked by sitting on black. If the tier needs to be visibly flagged as well, C is the next choice, because it sits furthest from the red and the two can never blur.
