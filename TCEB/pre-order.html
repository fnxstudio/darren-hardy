The premise

Your business is already compounding. The only question is: in which direction?

You probably cannot answer that. Almost nobody can.

Everything competing for your attention is new. Every quarter delivers another thing you are apparently already behind on. Meanwhile the fundamentals that built every enduring company go unattended inside almost all of them. Yours included. Nothing looks broken. That is what makes it dangerous.

Because your company never stands still. Every day it is becoming stronger, more valuable and harder to beat. Or more vulnerable to the next competitor, downturn or disruption. There is no neutral.

What is running underneath all of it is an operating system. You never sat down and wrote it. It was written by default, by the decisions you made twice, and it is compounding right now whether you are watching it or not. This book shows it to you, and hands you five upgrades to rewrite it.

Your company does not execute your intentions. It photocopies your decisions.

The author

Few people have seen that many playbooks

For more than 35 years, Darren Hardy has studied extraordinary achievement from the inside.

As publisher of SUCCESS magazine and executive producer of three television networks devoted to success, he sat face to face with many of the world's most accomplished founders, CEOs and business leaders. They opened their playbooks, revealing the decisions, disciplines, failures, systems and standards behind what they built.

Darren has also built, bought and sold more than a dozen companies of his own, and has mentored more than 11,000 CEOs and founders.

What he found was never a secret, a hack, or a moment. It was a short list of unremarkable disciplines, repeated long past the point where most people quit.
