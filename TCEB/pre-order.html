The operating system, and five upgrades

Every company is already running an operating system. Almost nobody wrote theirs on purpose. Chapter one shows you yours. The five that follow rewrite it, one upgrade at a time.

The governing equation
Smart decisions × repeatable systems × consistent routines × a reinforcing environment × time = an extraordinary enterprise.

Run the same equation with small errors in place of smart decisions and it produces enterprise decay at exactly the same speed.
