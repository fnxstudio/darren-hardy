01 · The grounds

Two grounds.
Nothing between them.

The jacket does not use a tinted paper, a cream, or a warm grey. It is pure white against pure black, and the hardness of that edge is the identity.

White
#FFFFFF
Flaps, back panel, the bands that frame the cover. Where the argument is made and the reading happens.
Black
#000000
The cover itself. Where the title lives, and the only ground gold is permitted on.
This corrects the earlier proposal. A warm paper at #fbfaf8 and a mist grey at #dcd9d5 were drafted before the jacket was available. They soften a system that is meant to be hard, and they cost contrast: the red measures 4.47:1 on warm paper, which fails body text, and 4.67:1 on pure white, which passes. The jacket is both more elegant and more accessible.
02 · The accent

One red. Once.

On the jacket the red appears on a single phrase per surface. IN BUSINESS on the cover. One sentence on the flap. One word in the URL. That discipline is the reason it lands.

Greys are type tiers,
not grounds.

White, light grey and silver belong in this system. They earn their place as levels of type and as rules, which is how the jacket uses them. They are not additional backgrounds.

04 · Type

Condensed caps.
Set tight. Set large.

The jacket runs one display face at one extreme and one body face at the other. The gap between them is the whole typographic idea. There is no middle.

05 · Composition

The band.

The cover's structure is a black field framed by two white bands. That device is the layout system, not just cover art. It gives a page its rhythm without a single extra colour.
