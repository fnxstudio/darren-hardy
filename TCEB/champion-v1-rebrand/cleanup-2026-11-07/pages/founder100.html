Exceptional businesses are not built by accident.

The Founder 100 recognizes leaders who have chosen to pursue that standard.

A Note to the Founders

To the Founder 100,

When I wrote The Compound Effect, I could never have imagined the impact it would have around the world.

Sixteen years later, The Compound Effect In Business goes to print, and your name goes with it, in its very first edition.

You made that decision before the first printing, while others waited to see what this book would become. And you didn't keep it to yourself. You put it into the hands of the people you lead and serve. That is what the exception does.

Your name will forever be part of this book's history. But what matters most is what it represents: a leader committed to building an exceptional business, and to becoming the exception in your market, your organization and your industry.

This next chapter is yours, too.

Be The Exception,

Darren Hardy
