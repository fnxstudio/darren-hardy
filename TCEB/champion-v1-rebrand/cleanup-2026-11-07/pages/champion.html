Questions.Answered.

Still stuck? Your Champion host is a reply away.

What is the Founders Champion Program?

It’s how Founders bring the leaders they respect to the Founders Event and earn their way into the Studio with Darren. Every seat credited to you is an impact on your Champion Board. The more impact you make, the higher you climb.

Does my own seat count?

Yes. Your own seat earns you one impact. Seats you invest in for your team count too, one impact per seat.

How do I earn more Impact Points?

There are 3 ways to bring a leader:

Gift + Invite: Share your personal link. We send the leader The Compound Effect, compliments of Darren, and personally invite them to the Founders Event on your behalf.

Team Seat: Secure a seat for someone on your leadership team, a key partner or a client at the Team Seat rate.

Hardy Exchange: Assign your credits to a seat for a leader you want to bring.

Every confirmed seat is one impact on your board.

What does a leader receive when they use my personal invitation link?

A private invitation to the Founders Event with a $1,000 scholarship toward their seat, plus the hardcover and audiobook of The Compound Effect, compliments of Darren.

How do I see where I stand?

Your Champion Board shows your rank, your Impact Points and how far you’ve moved this week. It updates within an hour of seat confirmation.

A leader I introduced says they enrolled, but it’s not on my board.

A seat is credited once it’s tracked to your link or verified by our team. If it isn’t showing within 24 hours, email helpme@darrenhardy.com with the leader’s name and email address, and we’ll verify it to ensure you receive your impact.

How are the In-Studio seats awarded?

There are 40 In-Studio seats. The top 20 Champions by impact at the final count earn their seats outright. The final 20 are randomly selected from every Champion with at least one impact on the board. Your own seat earns you one impact, so every Founder is eligible for the drawing.

Who earns a seat at Darren’s Table?

The top 5 Champions at the final count earn a private dinner with Darren and Georgia Hardy in Charleston, SC.

When is the final count?

The board closes on April 2, 2027, and the Champions earning In-Studio seats will be announced the week of April 9.

What if I earn a seat but can’t attend?

Let us know as soon as you can. In-Studio seats can’t be transferred or exchanged for cash, so your seat passes to the next qualifying Champion in line. You keep your Founders Event seat and join every session live on the broadcast.
