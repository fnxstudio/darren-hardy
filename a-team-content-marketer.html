The Position

You Own The Content Machine.

Darren has spent 35 years producing some of the most valuable business content on earth. Books. Keynotes. DarrenDaily. Thousands of hours of mentoring that's reshaped how the world's top leaders think and build.

That's the raw material. You build the machine that turns it into momentum. The workflows, automations, and agents that forge it into lead magnets, short-form video, and social proof, then push it to the people who need it most.

This isn't a content calendar to fill. It's a front-end engine to build, run, and obsessively improve. One that feeds every campaign and fuels the entire mission.

Why It Matters

Our work positively influences the lives of 1.6 billion people.

Every asset you ship is one more leader reached, one more business built, one more ripple in a mission far bigger than any single role. This seat sits at the front of all of it.

Who You Are

Not For Everyone.
Maybe For You.

We're after a proven content marketer with the receipts to back it. Read this list honestly. If most of it sounds like a description of you on your best day, we should talk.

  • 01 You write copy that makes people feel something, and do something. Persuasion is a craft you take seriously.
  • 02 You produce at high volume without dropping the quality. Output and excellence aren't a trade-off for you.
  • 03 You treat AI tools as a force multiplier, not a crutch. And you're already fluent.
  • 04 You're self-directed and iterate fast. You don't wait to be told. You ship, measure, and improve.
  • 05 You understand high-achieving leaders. How they think, what moves them, and why they buy.
  • 06 You're obsessed with getting better. Better Every Day isn't a slogan to you. It's how you're wired.
  • 07 You take total ownership of results. No excuses, no hand-offs. You own the number and the outcome.
  • 08 And the rare part: you do it with real EQ. You read people as well as you build and multiply systems.

This Isn't Your Seat If…

  • ×Your relationship with AI is asking it to write your emails and summarize your meetings. Fine upgrade. Wrong job.
  • ×You wait for a ticket, a tool, or permission before a problem gets solved.
  • ×You think "AI fluent" means knowing the names of the latest tools.
  • ×You'd rather perfect one asset by hand than build the system that ships a hundred.
